Privacy Policy

WordWise — Last updated: May 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how WordWise ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the WordWise mobile application ("App"). We are committed to protecting your privacy.

1. Information We Collect

WordWise does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on our own servers. All information you provide stays on your device.

The following data is stored locally on your device only:

  • Display name, email address, and age (entered during sign-up)
  • Password (stored locally; never transmitted to us)
  • Profile avatar photo (stored as encoded image data on your device)
  • Vocabulary progress: bookmarked words, learned words, and quiz history
  • App preferences: daily goal, reminder time, dark mode setting
  • Notification history (local notifications only)

2. Dictionary API

When you look up or load a word definition, the App sends the word text only (e.g., "ephemeral") to the Free Dictionary API (dictionaryapi.dev) to retrieve its definition, pronunciation, and examples. No personal information is included in these requests. Please review the Free Dictionary API's own privacy terms for their data handling practices.

3. Notifications

WordWise uses local notifications scheduled on your device to send daily word reminders. These notifications are generated entirely on your device and are not routed through any external server. You can disable notifications at any time in the App's Settings or in your device's system settings.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, rent, or share your personal information with any third parties. Because all data is stored locally on your device, it is not accessible to us.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

All app data is stored locally via your device's secure storage. You can delete all your data at any time by using the "Delete Account" option in the App (Profile → Account → Delete Account), or by uninstalling the App from your device.

6. Children's Privacy

WordWise is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe your child has provided information through the App, please contact us so we can remove it.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. Continued use of the App after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of those changes.
