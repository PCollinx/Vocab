## Full Description
WordWise helps you grow your vocabulary one word at a time — every day.

Each day you get a fresh set of words with clear definitions, example sentences, and audio pronunciation. Swipe through your daily word cards, then test yourself with a quick quiz to lock in what you've learned.

**Features:**
• Daily word cards with definitions, examples, and pronunciation
• Quiz mode to test your knowledge and track progress
• Streak tracking to keep you motivated
• Daily reminders so you never miss a day
• Dark mode support
• Sign in with Google or email

Whether you're preparing for an exam, sharpening your professional communication, or just love words — WordWise makes vocabulary building a daily habit.

Download WordWise and start building a stronger vocabulary today.
