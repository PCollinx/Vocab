### Full Description (max 4000 chars)

Build a richer vocabulary — one word a day.

WordWise makes learning new words feel effortless. Every day you get a curated set of words across categories like everyday language, academic writing, literature, and business — each with a full definition, pronunciation guide, usage example, synonyms, and antonyms. Swipe through your daily words, bookmark the ones you love, and test yourself with a quiz that adapts to what you've learned.

**DAILY WORDS**
A fresh carousel of words greets you every morning. Work through your daily goal at your own pace — whether that's 1 word or 10.

**SMART QUIZZES**
Multiple-choice questions test your definitions and synonyms. Your quiz history tracks how you improve over time.

**WORD LIBRARY**
Bookmark any word and find it instantly later. Search your saved words by definition, category, or part of speech.

**EXPLORE BY CATEGORY**
Browse curated word lists across 8 categories: Everyday, Academic, Literary, Business, Scientific, Technology, Arts, and Nature.

**STREAKS & ACHIEVEMENTS**
Stay motivated with a daily learning streak. Unlock achievements as your vocabulary grows — 50 words, 100 words, 7-day streak, and more.

**DAILY REMINDERS**
Set a reminder at the time that works for you. WordWise sends a gentle nudge so you never miss a day.

**CLEAN, DISTRACTION-FREE DESIGN**
Light and dark mode. No ads. No clutter. Just words.

---

WordWise is free to download. All your progress, bookmarks, and preferences are stored privately on your device.

## SCREENSHOTS

Capture these 6 screens in the simulator/device at the required size.
For App Store: 6.9" (iPhone 16 Pro Max) — 1320 × 2868 px is required.
For Play Store: any phone screenshot works; Play also wants a 1024 × 500 px feature graphic.

### Screenshot order & captions

1. **Home — Daily Words**
   Caption: "A new word every day"
   Screen: Home tab showing the daily word carousel in action.

2. **Word Detail**
   Caption: "Full definitions, examples & synonyms"
   Screen: Word detail modal showing definition, part of speech, pronunciation, example sentence, and synonyms list.

3. **Quiz**
   Caption: "Test yourself — and actually remember"
   Screen: Quiz screen mid-question showing multiple-choice options.

4. **Bookmarks**
   Caption: "Save the words that matter to you"
   Screen: Bookmarks tab with a list of saved words, search bar visible.

5. **Categories**
   Caption: "Explore 8 curated word categories"
   Screen: Category browse screen or a category word list.

6. **Profile & Streaks**
   Caption: "Track your streak and achievements"
   Screen: Profile tab showing streak count, achievements grid, and daily goal.

### Screenshot tips
- Use iPhone 16 Pro Max simulator for App Store (required for 6.9").
- Turn on dark mode for 2–3 of the 6 shots to show the dark theme.
- Make sure the status bar time shows something clean (e.g. 9:41 AM).
- In Simulator: Device → Screenshot (Cmd+S saves to Desktop).
